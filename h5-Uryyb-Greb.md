### Applied Cryptography (€ Schneier 2015: Applied Cryptography: Chapter 1: Foundations)

- Its about messages between a sender and a receiver and protecting the information in the message
- Plaintext versus Ciphertext (Plaintext is clear to read. Ciphertext is encrypted)
- Cryptography is the overarching term
- Cryptanalysis is the act of breaking encryption  

### PGP - Send Encrypted and Signed Message - gpg (Karvinen 2023: PGP - Send Encrypted and Signed Message - gpg)

 - PGP or gpg is a common an secure way to encrypt messages, especially email
 - PGP = Pretty Good Protection
 - gpg = GNU privacy guard
 - Uses public and private key pair to encrypt and decrypt messages
 - The article presents instructions on how to do it using linux command line even though thats not the most common usecase
